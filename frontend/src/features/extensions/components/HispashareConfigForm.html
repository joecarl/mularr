Adds the Hispashare catalogue as a search provider. Its releases are eD2k files, downloaded by aMule. The API allows 250 requests per hour: identical searches are cached for 15 minutes and background searches (Sonarr/Radarr sync) stop when fewer than 30 requests are left. The Test button validates the token and costs one request.

Polls the catalogue for its most recently updated titles and adds their releases of the last 30 days to the Torznab feed Sonarr/Radarr read on their RSS sync, next to what the wanted sync finds. Each poll costs up to 5 requests.
